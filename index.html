The three check levels

Different roles.
Different checks.

The level of check depends on the role and the relevant eligibility rules. Explore the basics, then get in touch if you’re unsure.

01

Basic DBS

A basic check shows unspent convictions and conditional cautions.

02

Standard DBS

A standard check includes spent and unspent convictions, cautions, reprimands and warnings, subject to filtering rules.

03

Enhanced DBS

An enhanced check includes the information available on a standard check and may include relevant local police information. Eligibility applies.

Helpful to know

Questions,
made simpler.

A few useful starting points. For guidance about a particular role, send us an enquiry.

What is the difference between the three DBS check levels?

Each level can disclose a different scope of information. Basic checks cover unspent convictions and conditional cautions. Standard and Enhanced checks have broader scope and are only available for eligible roles.

Can any role have an Enhanced check?

No. The role must meet the legal eligibility criteria for that level. The appropriate level depends on the duties and the applicable rules.

What information should I include in an enquiry?

Tell us your name, how to contact you, and a general description of the role or question. Don’t send identity documents or sensitive personal data through this form.

Can I check an application live on this website?

This website does not provide a live application status check. Please use the appropriate official or provider channel for an existing application.
